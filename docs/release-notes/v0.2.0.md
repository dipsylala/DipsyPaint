# DipsyPaint 0.2.0

DipsyPaint 0.2.0 is a big release: layers, text, every shape, the artistic brushes and more file formats. Installed copies update themselves.

## New

**Layers and files**

- **Layers.** Add, duplicate, merge down, hide, reorder and delete layers in the Layers panel on the right.
- **Layered project files.** Save as OpenRaster (`.ora`), an open format for layered images, to keep your layers. Other formats save a flattened picture.
- **More formats.** Open and save GIF and TIFF, open ICO and WebP, and save BMP as 256-colour, 16-colour or monochrome. DipsyPaint warns before a save that reduces colours.
- **Resolution (DPI).** Pictures keep their resolution when opened and saved, and the status bar shows it.
- **New image sizes.** File → New offers common sizes (screen, square, phone, A4 and US Letter at 300 dpi) or a custom size, with a button to swap width and height.

**Painting and drawing**

- **Artistic brushes.** Calligraphy brush, calligraphy pen, airbrush, oil brush, crayon, marker, natural pencil and watercolour, in the Brush tool's Brushes menu.
- **Opacity** for the pencil, brush and eraser.
- **Pen pressure.** With a pen tablet, pressing harder paints a wider stroke. Mouse painting is unchanged.
- **Text tool,** with font, size, bold, italic, underline and colour.
- **Every shape from Paint's gallery,** including curve, polygon, arrows, stars, callouts, heart and lightning. Outlines and fills can also be crayon, marker, oil, natural pencil or watercolour.
- **Custom colours:** an **Edit colours** dialog, and 10 custom-colour slots under the palette.

**Selecting and transforming**

- **Free-form selection, Select all (Ctrl+A), Invert selection (Ctrl+Shift+I) and transparent selection.** Free-form selections show their real outline.
- **Magic wand** in the Selection menu: selects an area of similar colour, with a tolerance slider. Paint doesn't have this.
- **Rotate to any angle** with the rotate handle or **Custom rotate**, and **skew** in the **Resize and skew** dialog.
- **Shift while dragging a corner** keeps a selection's or shape's proportions.

**Viewing**

- **View menu:** zoom, rulers (Ctrl+R), gridlines (Ctrl+G), status bar, and full screen (F11).
- **Magnifier tool:** click to zoom in, right-click to zoom out.

**Safety**

- **"Save changes?"** prompt before New, Open, closing or restarting to update, when the picture has unsaved changes. A `*` in the title marks unsaved changes.
- **Crash recovery.** While you have unsaved changes, DipsyPaint keeps a recovery copy and offers to restore it after a crash. [Privacy](https://github.com/dipsylala/DipsyPaint/blob/main/docs/privacy.md) explains where these copies are kept and when they are deleted.

## Changed

- **Selections now use three tools.** **Select** only makes a selection; dragging inside it starts a new one. **Move selection** moves just the outline. **Move selected pixels** moves, stretches and rotates the pixels. Pasting switches to Move selected pixels. In both Move tools, Shift keeps a drag straight, and the arrow keys nudge by 1 pixel (10 with Shift). This differs from Paint, where dragging inside a selection moves the pixels.
- **Movable toolbar.** The command bar wraps instead of cutting off controls. Drag any group by its ⠿ handle to any edge of the window, or use the handle's menu. **Reset layout** restores the default, and the layout is remembered.
- **File, View and Help** sit together at the start of the toolbar.
- Switching tools no longer moves the canvas, and the toolbar is more compact.
- Large operations that need more memory than your machine can spare are refused with a message instead of crashing.
- Dragging, rotating and stretching large selections and shapes is much faster.

## Known issues

- TIFF files are saved without transparency.
- After rotating a selection or shape, its frame stays upright around the content.
- Rotated text can be moved and restyled but no longer edited.
